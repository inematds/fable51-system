Start with the three numbers

The model improved. The system grew. No one proved that one caused the other.

This course doesn’t sell Fable 5.1 or take it apart. It teaches you to distinguish what’s official from what’s extracted, what’s a model from what’s a product, and what’s a price per token from what’s a cost per task.

Sources and limitations

Where the claims in this course come from

Official system prompt

Published by Anthropic for its web and mobile apps. Fable 5.1 · index of all versions.

Captured runtime (unauthenticated)

Public extraction of the complete prompt with memory, search, artifacts, and 46 tools, frozen on 02/09/2026. Repository. This course always calls it a "captured prompt".

Benchmarks and pricing

Product page from Anthropic (scores) and the official API pricing table. The cost-per-effort measurements are from the official cost optimization guide, based on Fable 5.

Limitations

The captured file is not authenticated. A tool definition doesn’t mean it’s available in your account. Coexistence doesn’t imply causation. The product may have changed after September 2026.
